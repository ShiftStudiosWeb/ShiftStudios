A neighbourhood sushi bar.

Village Sushi Bar serves the sushi and Japanese dishes people order again and again, from simple salmon nigiri to bento boxes, hot noodle bowls and a few Korean favourites. It's an easy place for a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a tray for the office.

Dining in or taking out, the menu stays friendly on the wallet, with most people spending $10 to $20.
